Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. As you use Spendly, we also store the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions, along with technical details like login timestamps needed to keep your account secure.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide the core functionality of Spendly: logging your expenses, generating spending summaries, and letting you sign in to your account. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in our database for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information from unauthorized access. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, after which your records will be permanently removed.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on trusted third-party services for functions such as hosting and email delivery. These providers only receive the minimum information needed to perform their function and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes. Spendly does not share your expense data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at support@spendly.app.
